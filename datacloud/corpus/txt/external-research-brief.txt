Company Overview

Blackbaud (NASDAQ: BLKB) is the world's leading provider of AI-powered solutions for the social impact sector —

including nonprofits, K-12 schools, higher education institutions, healthcare organizations, foundations, and corporations.

Founded in 1981 and headquartered in Charleston, South Carolina, Blackbaud serves tens of thousands of organizations

in more than 100 countries. Its platform empowers organizations to raise funds, manage donors, engage constituents,

and run their operations more efficiently through purpose-built, AI-first cloud solutions. 1 · prnewswire

Blackbaud completed its strategic pivot to a cloud-first, subscription model over the past decade, exiting legacy on-

premise products. Its business model is predominantly recurring SaaS revenue (~90%+) with a growing focus on payment

processing and financial services for nonprofits. In a major strategic shift, Blackbaud sold EVERFI on December 31, 2024

for a nominal amount, refocusing entirely on its core social impact software portfolio. 2 · blackbaud The company

turned GAAP-profitable in FY2025 and has repositioned its brand as "the leader in AI for social impact.

" 3 · stocktitan

Industry & Market Position

Blackbaud occupies a near-monopoly position in purpose-built software for the social impact sector. It is the dominant

player in nonprofit CRM (Raiser's Edge NXT) and financial management (Financial Edge NXT) for mid-to-large

organizations. The company benefits from:

●

Deep switching costs: Data depth, workflow integration, and staff training make migration extremely disruptive for

established nonprofits

●

Network effects: Sector intelligence benchmarking and peer fundraising data improve with scale

●

Vertical depth: No horizontal CRM (Salesforce included) can match the out-of-box fit for complex nonprofit

workflows like constituent stewardship, gift processing, and grant management

Key tailwinds include growing demand for digital fundraising, increasing focus on nonprofit operational efficiency, the

expansion of corporate ESG/CSR programs (YourCause), and the emergence of agentic AI as a top enterprise priority.

Headwinds include increasing competition from Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, Microsoft, and newer entrants like Virtuous

CRM, as well as revenue headwinds from the EVERFI divestiture.

Digital Transformation & Sales Process Signals

Blackbaud has been on a multi-year journey to transition its entire customer base from legacy on-premise products

(Raiser's Edge 7, Financial Edge) to cloud equivalents (Raiser's Edge NXT, Financial Edge NXT). This migration is both a

revenue opportunity and a competitive retention play.
