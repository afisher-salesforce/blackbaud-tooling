# Friday review — walkthrough script

**Audience:** Russ Tallon (Enterprise Architecture) + the A2R and I2R Value Stream Leads. **Framing:** this is a
*working discussion canvas*, not a pitch. The goal is to get Blackbaud reacting to and correcting the alignment, and
to leave with their view captured. Run time ~20–25 min.

## Before you start
- Open the site; click the header **Connected** indicator once — "BFF up, Trailhead catalog served, rationalization
  agent on the roadmap." Sets the honest tone: real app, nothing hidden.
- Dark/light toggle to match the room's screen.

## 0 · Set the frame (2 min) — Overview `/`
- Lead with the hero: **86 tools across A2R and I2R; the question is "which of these do you already own?"**
- **Read the amber disclaimer aloud.** This is the whole credibility move: "This alignment is *our* preliminary
  view, built fast from Christa's draft — it's almost certainly too black-and-white in places, and today is about you
  correcting it." Russ (EA) will trust the rest of the session because you said this first.
- Point at the alignment distribution counts: Native + Integrates = the consolidation story; Gap counts shown
  honestly on purpose.

## 1 · The heatmap (3 min) — Overview
- "Each dot is a capability, colored by draft alignment. Green/blue clusters = where Salesforce already plays;
  slate = genuine gaps."
- Note the A2R vs I2R split visually. Click a green dot to jump straight into a capability — show it's interactive.

## 2 · The shades-of-gray centerpiece (4 min) — `/capability/prospecting-sales-intelligence`
- **LinkedIn Sales Navigator.** This is the set-piece. Christa's draft said "Inconclusive." Our review says
  **Integrates**: Sales Navigator surfaces *inside* the Salesforce record, and Data 360 can unify its signal — you
  keep the LinkedIn graph (it rides your Microsoft EA) *and* get it in-platform.
- The point to land: "A yes/no verdict would mislead here. That's why the whole map is a 6-way spectrum, and why we
  want your input per capability."
- Type a note in **Discussion notes** live (e.g. "Russ: confirm whether goal is replace or surface in-platform").
  Reload the page — it persists. That's the capture mechanism.

## 3 · The strong consolidation story (4 min) — Capability Map `/map`
- Filter **Alignment = Native**. "These are capabilities you can run on the Salesforce investment you already hold."
- Open **Forecasting & Pipeline** (Clari): Native; note Clari's renewal is the near-term forcing function. Scroll to
  the **Trailhead rail** — "you already own this, and here's the enablement path." (Salesforce Forecasting, Pipeline
  Inspection.)
- Open **Conversation Intelligence** (Gong): deliberately **Partial**, not Native — "ECI covers the jobs, but
  unwinding Gong is a sales-process conversation, not a line-item swap. We're flagging that honestly."

## 4 · The honest gaps (2 min) — Capability Map
- Filter **Alignment = Gap**. "We're not claiming these — Adobe Creative Cloud, Getty, G2, Jira, Camtasia. Naming
  where Salesforce doesn't play is what makes the Native calls credible."

## 5 · Value-stream views (3 min) — `/a2r` and `/i2r`
- Hand each Value Stream Lead their stream. A2R lead: the Marketo question is genuinely unsettled (identity /
  personalization gap) — that's a Discuss, by design. I2R lead: Service Cloud Voice *integrates* Amazon Connect; the
  open question is whether Connect is direct or via a voice partner.

## 6 · Capture + close (2 min)
- Back to `/map` → **Export notes**. One click downloads everything captured today as Markdown. "This is how your
  input leaves the room — nothing's locked in a tool."
- Show the **roadmap tile** on Overview: "Next step after this: a headless Agentforce agent that answers this
  inventory in plain language, grounded in your Salesforce data and Data 360. The backend seam is already here; it
  turns on when you provision org access."

## Guardrails for the room
- If asked "is this reading our real contracts/spend?" → **No.** Renewal dates, ACV, users, utilization are
  deliberately not here; that's a follow-up data request after today.
- If asked "did Salesforce decide these verdicts?" → "These are our SE's starting view on top of Christa's draft —
  explicitly for you to correct. That's the point of the session."
- If a verdict is challenged → capture it in the note and move on. Agreement on the *process* matters more than
  winning any single cell today.
